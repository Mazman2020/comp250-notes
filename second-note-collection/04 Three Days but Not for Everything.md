# Note 4: Three Days, but Not for Everything

Quotation
---------

“On average, exploitation occurred just three days after public disclosure. The report
also found that 80% of tracked exploitation occurred within 14 days and 90% within 30
days” (Rapid7 Intelligence 2026, under “The exploitation window has narrowed, big
time”).

Commentary
---------

This looks like the next step after Mandiants numbers (32 days, then 5, then 3), but
the two companies measure differently so I cant line them up as one trend. The
percentages are the useful part though as about 90 percent of attacks happen before a company
that patches once a month would get to the fix. But nothing here is measured in hours.
Our hours claim comes from PHP2Shell, where Wiz researchers built working attacks that
fast (Ntsele 2026), so we need more evidence than one case.

Subject terms
---------

patch window; time-to-exploit; measurement; fixed patch schedules
