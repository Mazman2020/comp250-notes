# Note 10: A Person Makes Every Call

Quotation
---------

“The growth of AI-assisted research also means the PHP security team receives far more
reports than before. . . . AI helps the team validate reports and group reports sharing
the same root cause, but a person makes every call and works on every patch” (PHP
Foundation 2026, under “Building a sustainable professional security organization”).

Commentary
---------

This is the same problem Verizon describes (note 7), but from the side of the people who
write the fixes. AI helps find flaws but fixing them still takes a person one patch at
a time. Since July the Foundation has announced 15 flaws and dealt with more than 100
reports that were invalid or repeated or that couldnt be confirmed (PHP Foundation 2026).
Every one of those takes time from the same small team described in note 8. So AI that
“helps defenders” can actually slow down the defenders who write fixes. The good thing though is
that AI is already helping sort reports here. What I still need is evidence that AI
helps with fixing and installing patches and not just finding flaws.

Subject terms
---------

human bottleneck; AI-assisted discovery; open-source maintenance; patch deployment
