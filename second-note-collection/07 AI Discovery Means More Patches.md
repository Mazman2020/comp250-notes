# Note 7: AI Discovery Means More Patches

Quotation
---------

“Outside your own development pipeline, one recommendation is to prepare for a large
number of patches from coordinated disclosures of AI-augmented vulnerability discovery,
while making sure to inventory and minimize your internet-facing footprint. The
zero-days will keep being discovered and weaponized, regardless if fueled by tokens, by
coffee or most likely a mix of both” (Verizon 2026, 32).

Commentary
---------

This changed how I see the “AI helps defenders” half of our claim. We say AI helps
defenders by finding flaws before they are public. But every flaw AI finds turns into
another patch that someone has to install which can lead to more problems. So AI finding more flaws actually adds work
to the slowest step which is patching (note 6) and also the PHP Foundation saw the same
thing (note 10). Verizons advice to “inventory” systems also fits PHP2Shell where many
companies didnt even know PHP was hidden inside products they used (Ntsele 2026). 

Subject terms
---------

AI-assisted discovery; patch deployment; remediation backlog; disclosure
