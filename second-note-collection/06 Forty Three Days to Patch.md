# Note 6: Forty-Three Days to Patch

Quotation
---------

“There is also a worse result for the median time elapsed for a vulnerability to be
fully patched by detection, shown in Figure 13. Our new median time is 43 days, almost
two weeks longer than last year’s 32 days” (Verizon 2026, 17).

Commentary
---------

Put this next to Rapid7’s three days (note 4) and you can see the gap our claim is
about. Attackers got faster and companies got slower in the same year. Verizon also
found that only 26 percent of these flaws were fully fixed at all (Verizon 2026, 17).
These are flaws the US government already knows are being attacked so they should be
the most urgent and they still took 43 days. Also, the clock starts by detection
when a companys scanner finds the flaw and not so much when the flaw goes public, so the real gap
is even bigger.

Subject terms
---------

patch deployment; patch window; remediation backlog
