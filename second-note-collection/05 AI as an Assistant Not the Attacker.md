# Note 5: AI as an Assistant, Not the Attacker

Quotation
---------

“In Q3, 8% of the threat reporting tracked by Rapid7 cited AI as an operational
component. The research identifies a shift from using AI primarily to help draft content
or code toward supporting tactical decisions and execution within attack campaigns. In
the documented cases, human operators continued to select targets and determine
objectives, but relied on AI assistance for technique selection, target assessment, and
adapting when an attack step failed” (Rapid7 Intelligence 2026, under “AI is taking on a
more operational role in attacks”).

Commentary
---------

Eight percent sounds small, but I dont think AI is a small factor. The number only
counts reports that cited AI and attackers dont usually announce their tools, so the
real number is probably higher. The more important part is the list at the end. Humans
still pick the targets but the AI handles the steps in betweenw which is including “adapting when an
attack step failed.” That is what makes attacks faster. Normally a human hacker would end up maybe
geting stuck then work to figure out what went wrong, and try again, and AI skips most of that waiting.
So AI doesnt need to run the whole attack to shrink the time companies have to patch.
These numbers cover July to September, before PHP2Shell was announced on October 21.
