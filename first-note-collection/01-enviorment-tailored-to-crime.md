Title: An Enviorment Tailored to Crime

Quotation
----------
"It's almost like you're creating an environment tailored to crime."
(Dan Melzer, director of first-year composition, UC Davis)

Source
----------
Hsu, Hua. "The End of the Essay." New Yorker, July 7 and 14, 2025, 21–27.

Point locator
----------
p. 24

Commentary
----------
Melzer changes the problem from student character to the design of assignments.  He talks about cheating the way someone might talk about crime, like a someting that happens based on the conditions not just something bad that people do.
The conditions he lists are things like generic promps, no check ins, and long lenghts without due dates.
