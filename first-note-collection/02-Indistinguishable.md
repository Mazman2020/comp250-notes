Title: Students are indistinguishable within

Quotation
--------
"Students feel compelled to find distinction outside the classroom because they are largely indistinguishable within it."
(dean at Harvard that's paraphrased by Hsu)

Source
------
Hsu, Hua. "The End of the Essay." New Yorker, July 7 and 14, 2025, 21–27.

Point locator
-------------
p. 24

Commentary 
---------- 
I think that this is a part of the reason why students feel like they want a shortcut.
Almost eighty percent of Harvard's class of 2024 reported a GPA of 3.7 or higher so grades are barely separating anyone anymore.
When the work from courses can't make you stand out as a student it can just become something to finish and get done with.

Keywords
--------
incentives; student attitudes; coursework
