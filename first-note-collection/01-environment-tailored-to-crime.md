Title: An Environment Tailored to Crime

Quotation
----------
"It's almost like you're creating an environment tailored to crime."
(Dan Melzer, director of first-year composition, UC Davis)

Source
----------
Hsu, Hua. "The End of the Essay." New Yorker, July 7 and 14, 2025, 21–27.

Point locator
----------
p. 24

Commentary
----------
Melzer changes the problem from student character to the design of assignments.  He talks about cheating the way someone might talk about crime, like something that happens based on the conditions not just something bad that people do.
The conditions he lists are things like generic prompts, no check ins, and long lengths without due dates.

Keywords
----------
academic integrity; incentives; assignment design
