Title: Writing is thinking

Quotation
---------
"Writing is thinking... In fact there's a kind of thinking that can only be done by writing."
(Paul Graham)

Source
------
"Often Helpful, Sometimes Bad, Always Dangerous." Economist, February 15, 2025, 63.

Point locator
-------------
p. 63

Commentary
----------
I think that Graham's claim here is a good one and he's not trying to say that writing can help you think but rather that some sort of thinking its the only option.
And if we think that's true well then AI that is writing for you isn't just saving time and completing a step for you but its handicapping and cutting off the route to thinking. I also think that this can answer the earlier question in note 3 where the student did it right and asked what they got for it.
